Smart Cross-Docking

What it does

Smart Cross-Docking is the core module of MIXMOVE HUB OS, designed for zero-storage, high-throughput operations. It consolidates and reconstructs cargo at item and loading-unit level, so goods leave the hub with minimal dwell time.

For each load, business rules weigh weight, volume, destination, service level, time windows and handling constraints, and decide whether to consolidate or reconstruct. Rules can be configured per customer, lane or service.

In the module

Key capabilities

  1. 01Cargo consolidation and reconstruction
  2. 02Inbound planning and receiving
  3. 03Outbound planning and execution
  4. 04Documentation and labelling
  5. 05Discrepancy and exception handling
  1. 01

    Cargo consolidation and reconstruction

    • Rule-based decisions on consolidating or reconstructing at item and loading-unit level
    • Multi-parameter optimisation across weight, volume, destination, service level, time windows, handling constraints and customer rules
    • Pallet building and breaking, mixed SKUs, and destination- and service-based rules
    • Reconstruction rules, lane rules and loading list templates configured per customer, lane or service
  2. 02

    Inbound planning and receiving

    • Arrival management with ETAs, ASNs, time slots and dock pre-assignment
    • Mobile scanning on receipt to verify quantity, identity and condition
    • Capacity planning for inbound flows: docks, staff and equipment
    • Inbound receiving monitors that follow waves and exceptions in real time
  3. 03

    Outbound planning and execution

    • Load assignment based on destination, service level and cut-off times
    • Loading sequence optimised for route plans and time windows
    • Dock management for outbound waves, last-minute changes and re-allocations
    • Staging and marshalling visibility: where each loading unit is and what is missing
    • Outbound lane allocation and lane monitors to prevent misloads
  4. 04

    Documentation and labelling

    • Carrier, GS1-128, customer-specific and internal labels, with reprinting
    • Packing lists, manifests, bills of lading, and dangerous goods and cross-border documents
    • Versioned document archive with a full audit trail
    • Standard and custom label, POD and waybill templates
  5. 05

    Discrepancy and exception handling

    • Real-time capture of damages (with photos), shortages, overages and misrouted items
    • Configurable workflows for internal resolution and customer communication
    • Escalation paths and SLAs
    • Analytics across suppliers, carriers and sites
    • Damage reporting directly from the MIXMOVE HUB OS mobile app

Outcomes

Business value

What changes when goods flow through the hub instead of waiting in it.

  1. 01

    More throughput from the same space

    Consolidation and reconstruction at item and loading-unit level, with dwell-time control, keep goods moving through the hub. Volume grows without adding storage capacity.

  2. 02

    Fewer errors and misloads

    Mobile scanning on receipt, outbound lane monitors and real-time discrepancy capture catch problems at the stage where they occur, before they reach the next one.

  3. 03

    Customer and business rules applied consistently

    Reconstruction rules, lane rules and loading list templates are set per customer, lane or service, so every load follows the agreed rules.
